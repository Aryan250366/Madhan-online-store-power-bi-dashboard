# Madhan Online Store – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze the performance of an online retail store.  
The dashboard provides clear visibility into sales, profit, quantity sold, customer contribution, regional performance, product categories, payment methods, and monthly trends.

The purpose of this project is to convert raw transactional data into meaningful insights that can help businesses make data-driven decisions.

## Business Insights

### Customer Contribution Analysis
The dashboard shows that a small group of customers contributes a significant portion of total sales.

**Insight:**  
Identifying and targeting these high-value customers through personalized offers, loyalty programs, and exclusive deals can increase repeat purchases and long-term customer value.

---

### Regional Performance Analysis
Maharashtra is the top revenue-generating state, while Delhi also appears among the top-performing regions with strong growth potential.

**Insight:**  
- Maharashtra should be prioritized for marketing, inventory, and operational focus  
- Delhi can be targeted with region-specific campaigns to further increase sales  

---

### Payment Method Preferences
Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most preferred payment method, followed by UPI and other digital payment options.

**Insight:**  
High COD usage reflects customer trust preferences, while increasing UPI usage shows growing adoption of digital payments.

**Recommendation:**  
Offering incentives on digital payments can reduce COD dependency and improve cash flow efficiency.

---

### Category and Sub-Category Performance
The Clothing category contributes the highest quantity sold, indicating strong customer demand.  
Certain sub-categories such as Tables and Trousers generate higher profit margins.

**Insight:**  
- High-demand categories should be prioritized for inventory planning  
- High-margin sub-categories should be promoted more aggressively to improve profitability  

---

### Monthly Profit Trend Analysis
The month-wise profit analysis shows fluctuations, including months with negative profit.

**Insight:**  
These variations may be caused by seasonal demand changes or higher operational costs.

**Recommendation:**  
Targeted promotions and cost optimization during low-performing months can help stabilize profits.

---
